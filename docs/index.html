Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about setting up and using the app.

Can this get my WhatsApp account banned?

No. Unofficial WhatsApp bots that reverse-engineer the WhatsApp web protocol risk account bans. This app never touches WhatsApp's internal code or servers. It operates strictly via Android's official Notification Listener and RemoteInput APIs, identical to how an Android Auto car display or Galaxy Watch replies to chats.

How do I get a free Google Gemini API key?

Visit Google AI Studio (aistudio.google.com/app/apikey), sign in with your Google account, and click "Create API Key". Google provides free monthly quota that is more than sufficient for thousands of auto-replies.

Does it work with WhatsApp Business?

Yes! It works seamlessly with both WhatsApp and WhatsApp Business notifications.

My phone kills the app in the background. How to fix?

On Xiaomi, Samsung, Oppo, or Vivo devices, go to Settings → Apps → WhatsApp Auto-Responder → Battery, and set it to "Unrestricted" / "Don't Optimize", and enable "Autostart".
